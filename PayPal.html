Terms & Conditions
PayPal to M-PESA · Last updated 1 May 2026

1. The service

PayPal to M-PESA lets you move money from your own PayPal account to your own M-PESA wallet. Sending from M-PESA to PayPal is not offered.

2. Account linking

You may link only your own M-PESA number to your own PayPal account. The PayPal account holder name must match your M-PESA registered name. One PayPal account can be linked to one M-PESA number, and one M-PESA number to one PayPal account.

3. SIM swap

If your SIM was swapped in the last 48 hours, you must wait 24 hours before linking.

4. Fees and exchange rates

PayPal charges 3% per transfer. The exchange rate is set at the time of the transfer. The rate, fees and the amount you receive in ETB are shown before you confirm.

5. Limits

M-PESA customer transaction limits apply. Limits may change with regulatory requirements.

6. Security

Every withdrawal must be authorized with your M-PESA PIN. Never share your PIN.

7. Support

If money reaches the wrong account, contact M-PESA support so the case can be escalated.

8. Recycled numbers

If your M-PESA number is recycled, its PayPal link is removed automatically.

Unlink PayPal?

This disconnects your PayPal account from M-PESA. You can link it again anytime.
