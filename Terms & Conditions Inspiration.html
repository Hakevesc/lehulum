Terms & Conditions

Your approved Errif overdraft limit is 5,000.00 ETB. Please note that this limit is dynamic and may change based on your ongoing usage, repayment history, and overall account standing.
Outstanding amounts are due within 30 days of activation. To ensure seamless service, amounts may be recovered automatically from incoming funds (such as airtime purchases or mobile money deposits) until the balance is cleared.
Enjoy 0% interest for the first 7 days. A daily service fee applies thereafter as published in the official Tariffs & Fees schedule. Ensure timely repayment to avoid additional charges.
Errif is provided at the sole discretion of the service provider. The service may be suspended, or your limit reduced, if terms are not met, if payments are missed, or if account activity falls below required thresholds.
